Short-letting compliance

Short-term letting isn't a regulated industry

There's no mandatory regulation around short-term letting across most of the UK. That doesn't mean you should skip safety — the checks below are sensible advisory best practice: nice to have, not essential. There are just two exceptions:

Most of the UK
No regulation
The safety checks below are advisory only — not mandatory.
London
90-day rule
A 90-night annual cap applies. Stayful works around it and helps you stay compliant.
Scotland
Licence required
A short-term let licence is needed — we guide you through it.
Advisory safety checks · nice to do, not required
🔥 Fire safety
Smoke alarms Fire blankets Fire extinguishers Fire doors · nice extra
Outside London & Scotland, none of these are legal requirements for short-term letting — they're sensible safety steps we'd recommend, and we'll advise you on what's worth doing.
